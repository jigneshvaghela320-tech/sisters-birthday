# 👑 Happy Birthday Big Sister! - 6-Page Celebration Website

A heartwarming, interactive, and beautifully designed multi-page birthday website created specifically for an older sister.

---

## 🚀 How to Open and View

1. **Directly in your Browser**:
   - Double-click **`index.html`** or right-click `index.html` &rarr; **Open with Google Chrome / Microsoft Edge / Brave**.
   - No installation, node, or servers required! Everything works directly offline as pure HTML5/CSS3/JavaScript.

2. **How to Share with Her**:
   - You can zip this folder and send it to her, or drag-and-drop this folder onto [Netlify Drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop) or [Vercel](https://vercel.com) for a 1-click free live website link!

---

## 🌟 The 6 Interactive Pages

1. **[Home / Celebration Hub](index.html)**:
   - Festive hero artwork, floating balloons, sparkles, live birthday countdown clock.
   - Built-in **Web Audio API music player** that plays a sweet chime rendition of *"Happy Birthday To You"*.
   - Sister quote / compliment dispenser with confetti bursts.
   - Quick navigation gateway to all other chapters.

2. **[Memory Lane & Scrapbook](memories.html)**:
   - Polaroid-style photo gallery with washi tape details and hover tilt.
   - Categorized by: *Childhood*, *Late-Night Talks*, *Adventures*, *Silly Moments*.
   - Interactive Lightbox modal to read detailed stories behind every memory.
   - **`+ Add a New Memory`** button: Upload custom photos and write memories, stored in your browser's LocalStorage!

3. **[25 Reasons Why You're The Best Sister](reasons.html)**:
   - **Interactive Gold Scratch-Off Cards**: Rub with mouse or finger to scratch and reveal secret sibling confessions.
   - **25 3D Flip Cards**: Sibling reasons that flip with sound and confetti.
   - **"Surprise Me!"** button that randomly spins and highlights a reason.

4. **[Sisterhood Trivia Quiz](quiz.html)**:
   - 8-question sibling trivia game (*"How Well Do You Know Didi?"*).
   - Instant feedback, sound effects (correct chimes & funny buzzers), and score tracking.
   - **Official Printable Sisterhood Honor Certificate**: Generates a personalized certificate awarded to your sister that you can print or save as PDF!

5. **[Heartfelt Letter & Guestbook](wishes.html)**:
   - **Interactive 3D Wax Seal Envelope**: Click the seal to open and unfold a deeply moving personal sibling letter.
   - **Digital Wishing Wall**: Sticky note message board where family and friends can leave wishes with custom emoji badges, colors, and heart like counters.

6. **[Virtual Cake & Party Room](celebration.html)**:
   - **3D Birthday Cake**: Tap the candles or click *"Blow Out the Candles!"* to extinguish the flame with smoke animations, cheering fanfare, and a confetti cannon.
   - **Balloon Pop Arcade**: Click rising colorful balloons to pop them with sound effects and score points.
   - **Sibling Privilege Gift Box**: Click the ribbon to unwrap a golden coupon pass (*Free Boba/Treats & Chore Immunity*).

---

## ⚙️ Personalization: Make it Truly Hers

On **any page**, click the **`⚙️ Personalize`** button in the top navigation bar:
- Enter her **Actual Name** (e.g. *Pooja*, *Sarah*, *Didi*, *Ananya*)
- Enter her **Nickname**
- Enter her **Age / Milestone** (e.g. *25*, *30*)
- Enter **Your Sibling Name** (e.g. *Jignesh*)
- Set her **Birthday Date** for the countdown timer!

Once you click **Save & Apply**, every single page, quiz, memory header, and certificate will automatically update!
