To My Dearest Older Sister

Click the wax seal to unfold this personal sibling letter ✨

Dear Didi,

I don’t know who I would be today without you walking a few steps ahead of me, lighting the path.

Growing up, you were my bodyguard against the world, my shield when I made foolish mistakes, and the voice of calm whenever things felt overwhelming. Even when we bickered over trivial things, stolen t-shirts, or who took the last piece of cake, I always knew deep down that you loved me unconditionally.

You are ambitious, fiercely kind, hysterically funny, and stronger than you ever give yourself credit for. Watching you chase your goals inspires me to be a better person every single day.

On your birthday, I wish you peace of mind, wild adventures, boundless joy, and dreams fulfilled. May this upcoming year give back to you even half the warmth and kindness you pour into everyone around you.

Thank you for being my constant guide, my biggest cheerleader, and my favourite person to annoy! You deserve all the joy, love, and magic this world has to offer.

With all my love and endless admiration,
Your Little Sibling
